## Brand & Style

This design system translates the expressive, high-energy world of modern shonen and slice-of-life manga into an encouraging AI study companion. The personality is optimistic, supportive, vibrant, and approachable—designed to transform intimidating study sessions, test prep, and homework drills into an empowering comic book quest. 

The aesthetic blends **Neo-Brutalist Comic Graphicism** with clean **EdTech Utility**. Prominent visual hallmarks include crisp 3px deep navy ink linework (`#082A52`), tactile hard-edged offset drop shadows in vibrant cat-fur orange (`#FF8A00`), halftone screentone dots, energetic speed lines, and bubbly badge accents. High legibility balances expressive Japanese comic tropes: interactive forms, typography, and content containers maintain strict whitespace, impeccable readability, and distraction-free learning zones, reserving playful sticker decals, speech bubbles, and mascot moments for framing surfaces and milestone celebrations.

## Colors

The palette directly honors the energetic mascot palette: bright electric blue primary (`#0075E8`), warm feline orange secondary (`#FF8A00`), vivid comic sky tertiary (`#16B5F5`), and dark navy ink neutral (`#082A52`).

- **Primary (`#0075E8`)**: Anchors critical CTAs, active focus rings, key link destinations, and interactive highlights.
- **Secondary (`#FF8A00`)**: Powers the signature comic block shadows, badge accents, streak trackers, and mascot-themed highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#16B5F5`)**: Fuels decorative background halftones, speed lines, progress indicators, and radiant sparkles.
- **Neutral (`#082A52`)**: The foundational comic ink used for borders, structural outlines, crisp typography, and character linework, avoiding harsh pure black.
- **Named Accents**: 
  - `ribbon-red` (`#EF3E36`) adds character touches (Mika's bow) and urgency without conflating system error states.
  - `badge-cyan` (`#C5EFFF`) provides a cheerful, high-contrast tint for pill badges and micro-callouts.
  - `brand-yellow` (`#FFD34E`) drives reward stars, achievement badges, and textbook highlights.
  - `canvas-bg` (`#F8FCFF`) gives a soft, sky-tinted paper ground to maintain optical softness behind pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces.

## Typography

The core UI relies on **Nunito Sans** across all system levels to maintain rounded warmth, geometric balance, and high legibility during study sessions. 

- **Display & Headings**: Rendered in Heavy and Black weights (800–900) to emulate bold manga title lettering and punchy sound-effect typography, tightened with subtle negative tracking.
- **Body & Content**: Kept at regular to semi-bold weights with generous 1.5–1.6x line heights to prevent visual fatigue across practice questions and explanations.
- **Accent Comic Layering**: For speech bubbles, celebratory shouts, and mascot dialogue prompts ("Ayo belajar bareng!"), design implementations can inject decorative comic hand-lettering (such as Kalam or custom display vectors) alongside Nunito Sans labels.
- **Responsive Scaling**: Display and Large Headline sizes reduce down on mobile breakpoints to preserve viewport integrity and prevent text collisions in split-panel containers.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured **12-column fluid grid** nested inside max-width comic containers (1380px desktop cap). 

- **Canvas & Framing**: The backdrop features soft paper tone (`#F8FCFF`) garnished with SVG manga halftone dots and subtle dynamic speedline flourishes positioned strictly in peripheral zones to protect focus.
- **Split-Panel Architecture**: Two-panel hero containers divide layout space with a 53/47 proportion—allocating 53% to visual storytelling and companion interactions, and 47% to high-clarity transactional forms and reading matter.
- **Responsive Behavior**:
  - **Desktop (≥ 1200px)**: Side-by-side two-panel comic container framed with 3px solid navy borders, 64px internal padding, and full 14px hard orange block shadows.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: Proportions compress to 50/50, internal padding settles at 32px, and solid comic offsets reduce to 8px.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: Reflows into a single-column flow. The manga panel condenses into a compact header banner showing Mika's avatar and speech bubble, while the functional form or reading card moves to immediate focus with a 6px shadow offset and 16px horizontal margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons fuzzy, diffused realistic blurs in favor of **Graphic Neo-Brutalist Comic Stacking**:

- **Hard Block Comic Shadows**: Main cards, key containers, and prominent callouts feature a hard-edged solid orange shadow (`box-shadow: 14px 14px 0px #FF8A00`) combined with a crisp navy bounding outline (`border: 3px solid #082A52`). On mobile viewports, this shadow drops to `6px 6px 0px #FF8A00`.
- **Button Shadow Presses**: Primary interactive controls utilize a solid 4px navy or dark-blue offset shadow (`0 4px 0 #005FC4`). When pressed or active, the button translates down 2px (`transform: translateY(2px)`) with shadow reduced to 2px, providing an immediate tactile response reminiscent of arcade and pop-art physical buttons.
- **Interior Tonal Stacking**: Modals and nested interactive panels rest upon pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces bordered by subtle soft lavender dividers (`#D8DDF4`, 1.5px) to maintain internal calm inside bold outer comic shells.
- **Subtle Surface Floats**: Non-branded utility cards within content libraries or dashboard feeds apply crisp 1.5px borders and an ultra-subtle ambient lift (`0 4px 16px rgba(8, 42, 82, 0.06)`) to ensure high reading volume stays clean and fatigue-free.

## Shapes

The design system uses a pronounced, friendly rounded aesthetic calibrated across two distinct scales:

- **Large Shell Containers**: Main canvas cards, pop-out panels, and split-screen shells use large corner radii (28px to 32px), creating friendly, inviting comic vignette windows that soften the bold 3px ink borders.
- **Interactive Controls**: Inputs, buttons, and selectable list items use medium pill-like radii (12px to 14px), providing an ergonomic thumb-target that feels tactile and toy-like.
- **Status Pills & Speech Bubbles**: Badges, mascot dialogue tags, and pill chips utilize complete rounded caps (`border-radius: 9999px`). Mascot speech bubbles incorporate a directional triangular tail bordered in matching 3px navy ink.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid bright blue (`#0075E8`) background, crisp white Nunito Sans 700 label, 14px radius, 56px height. Features a solid 4px base shadow (`#005FC4`) with a quick 150ms press interaction shifting down 2px.
- **Secondary / Social Login**: Pure white background, 2px navy or muted border (`#082A52`), navy ink typography, centered authentic provider icons, 56px height.
- **Comic Accent Button**: Vibrant orange (`#FF8A00`) fill with 3px solid navy outline and crisp white text for milestone triggers and study gamification.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed in 12–14px rounded rectangles with 52–56px height, pure white surface, and 2px borders (`#D8DDF4`).
- Leading icons (such as envelope or lock) sit at 20px in muted ink (`#5868A4`).
- **Focus State**: Transforms border to crisp primary blue (`#0075E8`) with an outer 3px comic focus ring (`rgba(0, 117, 232, 0.25)`).
- **Error State**: Borders turn to danger crimson (`#BC2535`) accompanied by an alert icon and light pink alert callout (`#FFF0F2`).

### Comic Cards & Panels
- Constructed from a pure white canvas framed by a 3px solid navy ink outline (`#082A52`) and a solid 12–14px orange offset shadow (`#FF8A00`).
- Interior panel dividers utilize matching 3px navy vertical/horizontal strokes.

### Badges & Chips
- **Brand Hero Pill**: Soft cyan background (`#C5EFFF`), dark navy ink text (`#082A52`), uppercase tracking, framed with a leading star/sparkle icon. Fully rounded (9999px radius).
- **Subject Tags**: Light-tint backgrounds with matching saturated ink labels, providing effortless category distinction during quiz sessions.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 20px rounded squares (checkboxes) and circles (radios) with 2px navy borders. Checked state triggers an energetic primary blue fill displaying a crisp white hand-drawn style checkmark vector.

### Speech Bubbles & Mascot Callouts
- White surfaces bordered by 3px solid navy ink with a 16px corner radius and directional pointing tail. Designed to host Mika's encouraging quips, study advice, and step-by-step AI hints.
